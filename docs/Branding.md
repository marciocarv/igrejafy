# Branding

# IgrejaFy

> **Tecnologia para fortalecer igrejas.**

---

# Brand Purpose

IgrejaFy exists to simplify church administration so pastors and leaders can dedicate more time to people and less time to bureaucracy.

We believe technology should support ministry, not complicate it.

---

# Mission

Provide affordable, intuitive, and reliable technology that helps churches organize their ministry, care for their members, and grow with excellence.

---

# Vision

To become the leading church management platform in Brazil and Latin America, making professional management accessible to churches of every size.

---

# Core Values

## Ministry First

Every feature should help churches care for people.

Technology is never the goal.

It is the tool.

---

## Simplicity

Software should be easy enough for any church secretary, volunteer, or pastor to use.

Complexity is the enemy.

---

## Excellence

Simple does not mean basic.

Every screen should feel polished, professional, and reliable.

---

## Accessibility

Small churches deserve the same quality software as large churches.

Affordable pricing is part of our mission.

---

## Trust

Church data is sensitive.

Security and privacy are fundamental.

---

## Continuous Improvement

We build.
We listen.
We improve.

Every version should make churches happier.

---
