# IgrejaFy SaaS Roadmap

> "Tecnologia para fortalecer igrejas."

---

# Vision

IgrejaFy is a modern SaaS platform built to help churches of every size organize their administration, care for their members, and support their ministry.

Our mission is to make professional church management accessible to every church, regardless of its financial resources.

---

# Principles

Every new feature must satisfy at least one of these principles:

- Simplicity
- Reliability
- Security
- Affordability
- Ministry First

Technology should reduce bureaucracy so church leaders can dedicate more time to people.
