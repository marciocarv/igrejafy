Our Purpose

IgrejaFy exists to make church management simple, accessible and modern.

We believe that churches should spend more time caring for people than organizing paperwork.

Technology should support ministry—not complicate it.

The Problem

Thousands of churches still manage information using:

Paper notebooks
Printed member records
Excel spreadsheets
WhatsApp messages
Expensive or outdated systems

As churches grow, information becomes fragmented, making it difficult to care for members, follow up with visitors, and make informed decisions.

Our Solution

IgrejaFy centralizes everything in one platform.

Instead of several disconnected tools, churches have one secure, intuitive system where they can manage their ministry efficiently.

Our Mission

Empower churches through affordable technology that strengthens ministry and simplifies administration.

Our Vision

Become the leading church management platform in Latin America by making high-quality technology accessible to churches of every size.
